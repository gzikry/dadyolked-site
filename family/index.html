Join your family on DadYolked.

You’re one quick install and one Apple share acceptance away from helping with shared care.

Step 1

Install the DadYolked app

If you don’t have DadYolked installed yet, get it from the App Store. DadYolked is built for iPhone and keeps private dad journal and mood notes separate from shared care.

Download on the App Store
Step 2

Join your family

When you launch DadYolked, use the Apple Account that received the invitation. DadYolked will show the shared-care invitation so you can choose to join.

Already have the app?
  1. Open the DadYolked invitation from Messages or Mail.
  2. Accept the Apple iCloud share on the iPhone signed into the invited Apple Account.
  3. Return to DadYolked and start helping with feeds, sleep, diapers, and shared care notes.

The invitation link from Apple is what grants access. This page and the email that brought you here are instructions only. Private dad journal, mood check-ins, and profile settings stay private.
