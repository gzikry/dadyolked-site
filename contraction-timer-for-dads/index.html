What to track

Duration

How long each contraction lasts: start time to end time.

Frequency

How far apart contractions are: start of one contraction to start of the next.

Pattern + notes

Intensity, changes, fluid, symptoms, movement, and what the care team told you to do.

Contraction timer FAQ

What should dads track during contractions?

Track when each contraction starts and ends, its duration, start-to-start spacing, relevant changes, and the care team’s instructions.

Does a contraction timer tell us when to go to the hospital?

No. A timer records a pattern; it cannot assess labor or replace instructions from your OB, midwife, hospital, or other qualified care professional.

Does the timer save or send contraction data?

No. This practice timer runs in this browser tab and does not save, upload, or send its entries. Clearing the page or closing the tab removes the temporary list.
