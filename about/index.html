About DadYolked

Built for dads doing the work.

DadYolked is an independent iPhone app built and supported by George Zikry. It gives expecting and new dads one private place to prepare for birth, run the newborn shift, keep useful records, and remember that dad is a person in the room too.

The job

Make the next useful action obvious when somebody is tired, holding a baby, or trying to answer a care-team question without guessing.

The boundary

DadYolked organizes information. It does not diagnose, prescribe, determine medicine doses, or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

The privacy stance

Core records stay on the device by default. The app does not require a new account for basic tracking, and it does not use an advertising or data-broker business model.

The dad stance

Dad is not an optional accessory. The product covers pregnancy prep, baby-care handoffs, quick logging, and dad recovery without turning family life into another dashboard job.

How the website is maintained

Product claims are checked against the shipping app. Health-adjacent guides use named sources and clear safety boundaries. Material corrections are made directly, and the standards are public.
