Hospital bag FAQ

When should a dad or birth partner pack a hospital bag?

A practical target is to have the main bag ready by about week 36, while following your pregnancy care team's advice for your circumstances. Keep last-minute items—phone, wallet, daily medicine, glasses—on a short written list.

Should dad pack a separate hospital bag?

A separate small bag or clearly divided section can keep chargers, food, layers, toiletries, documents, and support items easy to find without disturbing the birthing parent's things.

What should we check with the hospital before packing?

Confirm visitor and overnight policies, food rules, parking, available toiletries and linens, device charging options, and what the hospital supplies for the baby. Hospital policy and the birthing parent's preferences come first.
