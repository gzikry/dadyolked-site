Four privacy pillars

Your family data is not the product.

Local by default. A partner sees only the baby logs you explicitly share.

01

Local-first, on device

Records live on your iPhone in SwiftData by default. Core tracking works with no account.

02

Optional private iCloud

FAQ

Before you download.

Pricing, what a partner can see, and what the app will not pretend to be.

What is free in DadYolked?

Core tracking and Ask DadYolked are free. That covers the stage-aware Today briefing, the 3 AM SOS sheet, the Labor 5-1-1 / 4-1-1 Command Center, and one-tap Feed, Sleep, Diaper, and Medicine logs. No account is required.

What is Ask DadYolked?

Ask DadYolked is free on-device Q&A. On supported iPhones it uses Apple Intelligence. Answers are clinician-anchored, show AAP, ACOG, CDC, and WHO source citations, and include a tappable medical disclaimer. It helps you prepare questions. It does not diagnose or replace your clinician.

What does DadWingman cost?

DadWingman is optional. It is $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year in the US App Store; local prices can differ. It unlocks Weekly Digest, Tonight Mode, 1-tap Partner Handoff, the Pediatrician Prep PDF packet, and Memory. Manage or cancel it in Apple ID Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

What syncs if I invite a partner?

Partner sync is off until you turn it on. Optional private iCloud shared care uses Apple CKShare and only the allowlisted baby logs: feeds, diapers, sleep, medicine, and growth. Private dad check-ins, journals, mood logs, HealthKit reads, and Assistant memory stay on your device.

Does DadYolked track me for ads?

No. DadYolked ships zero ad trackers, zero data brokers, and zero third-party analytics SDKs. Crashlytics is used for crash reporting only.

Is DadYolked medical advice?

No. DadYolked organizes logs and clinician-anchored references so you can ask better questions. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Health decisions belong with your clinician.
