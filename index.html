For new and expecting dads

What to do on your shift.

Today’s plan, a quick log for feeds, diapers, and sleep, and the words to use when you are not sure.

Free to download. Ask DadYolked is included and stays on your phone. DadWingman is optional: $39.99/yr or $4.99/mo, with a 1-week free trial.

The 3 a.m. log is three buttons.

Today is the plan for the stage you are in. Under it, Feed, Sleep, and Diaper are one tap. Medicine is there too. The log is a record, not a dose.

If your hands are full, you can log with Siri. DadYolked does not keep the recording. Apple may process the speech on the phone or on Apple’s servers, depending on the device.

Appointments live in the app, so the pediatrician question about wet diapers is something you can look up in the parking lot. If you are still pregnant, Today follows the week. The contraction clock times labor. It does not tell you when to leave.

What to say, and what to skip.

Dad Guides is a short script for the moment you are in. At the birth center it is the sentence that helps, and the one that will not. Later it is the practical thing: ice chips, the next feed, who is taking this stretch.

Journey is the stage guide next to that. Bonding, sleep, play, and when to call someone. It is general information. It is not a diagnosis, and it does not hand you a kick target.

One button: Invite your partner.

The share goes through iCloud. Your partner installs DadYolked and accepts Apple’s invitation on their iPhone. They do not need DadWingman to see the shared log.

What they can see: feeds, diapers, sleep, medicine, and growth. What stays on your phone: your Me check-in, journal, mood, Health data, and Ask DadYolked memory.

The same Care screen is where you take the next stretch, when your partner needs the break and you need the baby.

DadWingman is optional.

The Today plan, the logs, Dad Guides, and Invite your partner work without it. Ask DadYolked, the free assistant, also works without it. Ask DadYolked runs on your iPhone: Apple’s on-device model, or a built-in library if that model is not available. Nothing from it is sent to an AI service.

DadWingman is the paid tier. It adds Tonight Mode, Weekly Digest, Partner Handoff, Pediatrician Prep, and Memory. The assistant itself is Ask DadYolked, and that stays on the phone.

Read the privacy policy

Before you install it.

Is DadYolked free?

Yes. Today, quick logs for feeds, diapers, and sleep, Dad Guides, Invite your partner, appointments, and the Me check-in are free. Ask DadYolked, the on-device assistant, is free too. No account. DadWingman is the optional paid tier.

What does DadWingman cost?

DadWingman is optional. In the US App Store, Annual is $39.99 a year and Monthly is $4.99 a month. Both include a 1-week free trial. Local prices can differ. It adds Tonight Mode, Weekly Digest, Partner Handoff, Pediatrician Prep, and Memory. Apple bills you. Cancel in Apple ID Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Does my partner need the app?

To see the shared log, yes. They install DadYolked and accept the iCloud invite. They do not need DadWingman for feeds, diapers, sleep, medicine, and growth. Your Me check-in, journal, mood, Health data, and Ask DadYolked memory stay on your phone.

Where does my data go?

On your iPhone. If you tap Invite your partner, the baby’s feeds, diapers, sleep, medicine, and growth sync through your iCloud. The app sends crash diagnostics to Firebase Crashlytics: crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data, linked to the device and not used for tracking. No ads. This website does not use analytics. The privacy policy has the rest.

Is this medical advice?

No. DadYolked is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Guides and logs are general information. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States). Health decisions belong with your clinician.
