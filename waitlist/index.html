DadYolked is coming to the App Store.

The stage-aware pregnancy and baby tracker built for dads — not a pink app with a blue theme. It is in final release now; the download link on this site will go live the moment it appears in the App Store.

What it does

Quick logs for feeds, bottles, diapers, sleep, medicine and notes. Private iCloud sharing with the other caregiver. Widgets, Siri shortcuts and Apple Watch quick actions. A pediatrician-ready report you can hand over at the visit.

What it costs

Free Ask DadYolked and quick logs. Optional DadWingman is $4.99/month or $39.99/year. Local-first, and nothing is sold.
