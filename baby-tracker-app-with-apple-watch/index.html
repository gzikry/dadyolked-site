Best-fit use cases

Feeds and bottles

Start or save the bottle before the amount evaporates from your brain. Then use the iPhone app for fuller notes and patterns.

Diapers

Wet, dirty, mixed, and timing matter most when a pediatrician asks. Watch logging makes it less likely you miss the boring-but-important stuff.

Sleep starts and stops

Sleep logs are fragile because everyone is tired. The easier the start/stop action, the better the history.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Why use Apple Watch for baby tracking?

Apple Watch keeps common care actions closer when your phone is across the room, your hands are full, or you are logging at night. It works best for quick actions like bottles, diapers, sleep, medicine, and notes.

Should a baby tracker still work on iPhone?

Yes. Watch logging is fastest for quick events, but the iPhone app should remain the main place for review, search, history, partner handoffs, and pediatrician-ready records.

Does DadYolked require an account for core tracking?

No. DadYolked is privacy-first and does not require an account for core local tracking.
