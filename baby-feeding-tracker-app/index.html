What to log

A good feeding tracker answers the handoff questions.

What happened?

Record bottle amount, nursing side or session note, pumping amount, formula or milk prep notes, and whether the baby finished or refused.

When did it happen?

Use timestamps for feeds, naps, diapers, and medicine so you can see the pattern instead of guessing from vibes and half-sleep math.

What changed?

Log unusual fussiness, spit-up, poor feeding, sleepy feeds, latch issues, or anything your pediatrician asked you to watch.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Can dads track breastfeeding?

Yes. Dads can record session time, side notes, pumping, bottles, caregiver handoffs, and questions without replacing the feeding parent’s judgment.

Does DadYolked give feeding medical advice?

No. DadYolked is for records and planning. Use your pediatrician or lactation consultant for feeding concerns.

Why not use a notes app?

Notes work until you need patterns. A baby tracker keeps feeds, diapers, sleep, medicine, and handoffs searchable in one flow.

Does the printable feeding worksheet save what I write?

No. The blank worksheet on this page runs in your browser and does not save, submit, or upload anything you write. Printed notes and app exports can still contain private family health information, so store them securely and share only through channels your care team accepts.
